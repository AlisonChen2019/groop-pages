How to Use Groop

  1. Open a conversation in iMessage
  2. Tap the + icon in the bottom left and select Groop
  3. Enter an event name and set the date and time windows
  4. Send the message to schedule the event with your group
  5. Tap on the sent message to add your specific availability and view combined calendar
